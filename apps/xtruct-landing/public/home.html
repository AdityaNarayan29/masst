The map

It draws your product before it builds it.

The architect maps every service and request path, so you can see the system before you approve it. Each epic gets a map of its own.

The team

You hold one seat. Agents hold the rest.

Work splits from you down to the developers, then merges back through review, CI and a merge gate before it reaches main.
